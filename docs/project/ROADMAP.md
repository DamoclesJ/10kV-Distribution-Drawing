### Phase 3 — Confirmation + CommandStack (VERIFIED / ACCEPTED)

Accepted candidate: `2444fdf8a7e4e19440a3054fcc3fa0bcfae9c4ab`. The Confirm authority is the current valid EA result projected to a transient WorkScope Candidate; confirmation requires structural equality between the reviewed Candidate and the Candidate reprojected at planning time. No-current, stale, failed, no-seed, empty, mismatched, diagnostic-blocked, or ambiguous ticket/scope states are rejected without Domain, Ticket, or history mutation.

Candidate Region TerminalIds and ElectricalNodeIds materialize directly into persisted WorkScope Regions. Candidate Boundaries materialize as Domain `WorkScopeBoundary` values, supporting multiple Regions, zero or multiple Boundaries, RingCabinet, Pole, and CustomerStation cases. Confirmation does not fall back to manual Boundary A/B selection or create WTA `IsolationBoundary` values.

The accepted ticket linkage is one WorkTicket to one Confirmed WorkScope, containing 1..N Regions. First confirmation may attach a new scope to an existing ticket or create a ticket, scope, and linkage together. Exclusive re-confirm keeps the WorkScopeId, replaces its Region/Boundary snapshot, preserves Description and other ticket facts. Shared re-confirm forks a new scope for the confirming ticket while leaving the shared scope and other ticket unchanged. Tickets already holding multiple WorkScopeIds are rejected without guessing or merging.

One Confirm is one atomic `ConfirmWorkScopeCommand` and one CommandStack history entry. It captures complete Before/After snapshots. Undo/Redo replay those snapshots and stable TicketId/WorkScopeId values without rerunning EA or Candidate projection; injected partial Execute/Undo/Redo failures restore the corresponding full state. `AffectsEnergization = false`; Confirm, Undo, and Redo preserve the current EA result. Phase 3 changes only `WorkScopeIds` and the corresponding Domain WorkScope aggregate; all other existing ticket facts remain unchanged.
